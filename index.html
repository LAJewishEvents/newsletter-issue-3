A note from Andrew
Pesach is right around the corner — hope everyone is staying cool 🥵 as LA turns up the heat.

There are some great events coming up over the last two weeks of March, from Shabbat dinners and speaker nights to Passover learning and community gatherings across the city.

We’re continuing to track events across LA and build tools to make it easier to find the right vibe, region, and holiday happenings as Pesach gets closer.

In the meantime, here are a few standout events to keep on your radar.
— Andrew
